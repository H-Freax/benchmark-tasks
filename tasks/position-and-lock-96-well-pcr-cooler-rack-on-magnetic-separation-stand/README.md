### Task Procedure
In high-throughput genomic sequencing (NGS) library construction, clinical molecular diagnostics (e.g. viral RNA/DNA extraction), and solid-phase reversible immobilization (SPRI) magnetic bead cleanup protocols, reaction mixtures held in chilled 96-well skirted PCR cooler racks are routinely transferred onto high-gradient neodymium magnetic separation stands to immobilize magnetic beads along the well walls. A sliding mechanical lock bar is engaged over the skirt rim to rigidly retain the rack against strong magnetic pull-down forces, ensuring uniform bead ring pelleting and preventing plate displacement during automated washing steps.

1. **Grasp & Lift**: Manipulator (Left arm) pinches the chilled 96-well skirted PCR cooler rack (127.8 x 85.5 x 28.0 mm, 110 g) by its authentic perimeter skirt flange and lifts it from the benchtop silicone cleanroom staging mat at X=0.260 m, Y=0.200 m.
2. **Transfer & Registration**: Manipulator transfers the cooler rack across the bench and seats it squarely flush into the recessed registration nest of the 96-well magnetic separation stand (145 x 105 x 35 mm) centered at X=0.280 m, Y=-0.040 m (horizontal alignment error <= 20 mm, vertical flush <= 18 mm).
3. **Engage Slide Lock**: Manipulator (Right arm) pushes the knurled handle tab of the sliding lock bar forward along +Y (travel >= 8.0 mm) to rigidly clamp the cooler rack skirt against the stand's fixed perimeter stops, ensuring uniform magnetic bead pellet separation and preventing plate movement.
4. **Retract & Hold**: Both arms retract clear (>50 mm) for a continuous 20-step (1.0 s at 20 Hz) stillness hold.
