# Clear Metal Tongs from Commercial Panini Press Before Closing Heated Grooved Platen

## Summary
In high-volume fast food and commercial food service (BLS SOC 35-3023: Fast Food and Counter Workers, 3,460,000 workers; BLS SOC 35-2021: Food Preparation Workers, 891,000 workers; total >4.35 million US workers), commercial panini presses and grooved contact grills (Waring Commercial / Vollrath standard) are constantly operated under strict OSHA kitchen safety rules. Forgetting stainless utility tongs on the heated lower grill plate before closing the 7 kg spring-counterbalanced cast iron upper platen (450°F) crushes heating elements, bends platen hinge linkages, and causes severe thermal grease splatter. Workers must verify the lower grill is clear by removing metal utensils onto a side rest tray before lowering the platen into cooking position.

## Mechanics & Physics
- **Commercial Panini Press**: Heavy tabletop unit (280 x 300 x 190 mm, 7.5 kg) at `(0.280, 0.040)` m on prep counter (`Z = 0.7475` m). Lower grooved plate (`Z = 0.8125` m). Upper platen articulated via revolute joint with torsional counterweight resistance, starting propped open at 75 degrees with an insulated tubular front handle at `(0.420, 0.040, 0.945)` m.
- **Stainless Utility Tongs**: 240 mm scalloped utility tongs (180 g) resting across the lower grill surface.
- **Side Staging Rest Tray**: Heavy-gauge stainless tray at `(0.280, 0.260, 0.760)` m with 15 mm perimeter lip.
- **Bimanual Coordination & Safety Sequence**:
  1. Right arm grasps the stainless utility tongs by the grip handle, lifts them vertically clear of the lower grill plate (>50 mm), and places them stably onto the side staging rest tray (>80 mm from grill perimeter).
  2. Left arm reaches to the insulated platen front handle (avoiding heated grill plates), firmly grasps the handle, and smoothly pivots the counterbalanced upper platen downward 75 degrees until resting flush on the lower mechanical stop (`gap < 4 mm`).
  3. Left gripper releases the insulated handle, retracts both hands clear (TCPs >= 45 mm, hulls >= 5 mm clear), and sustains stillness for >= 20 steps (1.0 s).
- **Hard Safety Defect Interlocks**:
  - Closing platen while tongs remain on lower grill -> instant permanent 0.0 defect!
  - Gripper fingers contacting heated grill surfaces -> instant permanent 0.0 burn hazard defect!

## Stages & Scoring
- **0.20**: Metal tongs grasped and lifted clear of lower grill plate (>50 mm).
- **0.50**: Tongs safely deposited onto side staging rest tray (>80 mm clear of grill perimeter).
- **0.75**: Left gripper grasps insulated platen handle and rotates platen downward past 45 degrees.
- **0.90**: Upper platen fully closed flush on lower mechanical stop (<4 mm vertical gap).
- **1.00**: Upper platen flush, both hands retracted clear (>45 mm), and assembly held stationary for >= 20 steps (1.0 s at 20 Hz).
- **Defect Latches (0.0)**: Platen closed while tongs on grill, burn hazard contact, dropped tongs, or hands not clear during hold.
