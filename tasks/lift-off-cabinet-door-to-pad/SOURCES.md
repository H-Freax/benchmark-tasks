## Construction cut-list

Door panel: 350 mm along local y, 400 mm along z, 18 mm along x. One ordinary horizontal pull at 200 mm above its bottom. Two hinge shoulder planes at 50 and 310 mm above the panel bottom (260 mm spacing, 50 mm bottom offset and 90 mm top offset). At the 90-degree-open start the moving leaf is mounted to the door back face. The fixed leaf and its lower shoulder web connect to the near cabinet side; both real pins point up. The simple open carcase is 240 mm deep, 391 mm wide and 420 mm tall, 18 mm plywood, clamped/screwed to the bench. No cabinet contents are included. Reproduce the door and small cabinet at these actual dimensions, rather than scaling a full-size door down. Mount the lift-off pair in the correct handed orientation for upward removal.

The green destination is a 140 x 410 x 4 mm nonslip rubber bench pad taped flat to the tabletop. This is a cut rubber sheet, not a rack or custom cradle. Contacts are rigid with estimated friction; material elasticity is unmodeled.
