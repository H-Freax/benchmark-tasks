# Insert a roadside sign stand mast upright into its dual-spring base receiver and engage the spring lock pin

## Task Overview
A bimanual roadside safety hardware task: insert a commercial 1.25" square aluminum mast upright post (Dicke DL1000 / Cortina 03-800 standard lower receiver section, 140 mm length, 0.175 kg) into a dual-spring sign stand tubular steel receiver socket (Dicke DF3000 / Cortina 03-800 commercial base) on a highway work zone equipment bench. One hand retracts and holds a 40 N/m spring-loaded auto-locking pull pin outward, the other hand carries and inserts the mast upright 20 mm down into the 1.5" receiver socket until seated flush against the internal stop floor, the pin is released to snap its 3/8" hardened pin tip through the mast's factory locking hole, and positive anti-blowout retention is verified under an automated 25 N vertical proof pull.

## Real-World Objects & Sourcing
1. **Dicke DF3000 / Cortina 03-800 Dual-Spring Stand Base**:
   - Steel baseplate (100 x 100 x 10 mm) fixed to bench at (0.280, -0.060, 0.7475 m).
   - Dual wind-deflection coil springs flanking the mast base.
   - Receiver socket: 1.50" (38.1 mm) outer square steel tube, 20 mm tall, inner throat 1.42" (36.0 mm x 36.0 mm), socket floor at z 0.7575 m.
   - Auto-locking pull pin: 3/8" (9.5 mm) zinc-plated steel pin on a horizontal slider on left face (+y) at z 0.7725 m (15 mm above socket floor). 20 mm stroke with a 40 N/m compression return spring and knurled pull ring.
2. **Dicke DL1000 / Cortina 03-800-01 Mast Upright Post**:
   - 1.25" (31.75 mm) outer square aluminum tube, 140 mm length (5.5 in lower receiver stub), mass 0.175 kg. True 1:1 commercial off-the-shelf component.
   - Factory pre-drilled 7/16" (11.1 mm) locking hole centered 15 mm from the bottom end along y-axis, aligning with the base receiver pin when seated.
   - Universal sign clamp collar at top (46 x 46 x 25 mm) with dual knurled clamp screws.
   - Starts upright in a transport rack collar at (0.280, -0.180).

## Scoring Stages
- **0.3 (Partial Seating)**: Mast upright entered at least 15 mm into receiver socket (depth >= 15 mm).
- **0.7 (Full Seating & Pin Engaged)**: Post bottom within 25 mm of socket floor (tilt <= 12 deg) and spring pin released into locking hole (displacement <= 3.5 mm).
- **1.0 (Proof Retention & Hands Clear)**: Both hands clear (> 25 mm margin) and assembly withstands a 25 N vertical proof pull for 0.5 s with vertical displacement under 2.5 mm (held 1.0 s).
- **0.0 (Latched Failure)**: Dropping the post off the bench, tipping > 35 degrees, or releasing the pin while unseated causing the pin to jam against the solid tube face in the throat.
