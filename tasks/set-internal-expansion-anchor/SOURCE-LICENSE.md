# Sources and license

The original geometric solid definitions, engineering equivalent, reference controller and scorer are distributed under the included MIT license. The generic task adapter retains its MIT notice in `native/LICENSE`. No downloaded CAD, manufacturer drawing, manual, product photograph or material-data PDF is redistributed.

The exterior dimensional class is inspired by commercial 3/8-inch drop-in anchors. The split sleeve, reliefs, cone, punch, hammer, supports and pedestal dimensions are original manufacturing definitions. This is an engineering equivalent, not an OEM internal CAD replica or rated fastening product.

Reference-only sources:

- Simpson Strong-Tie, DIABST-SDS setting-tool product information: https://www.strongtie.com/mechanicalanchoringaccessories_mechanicalanchoringproducts/diabst-sds_tool/p/diabst-sds-plus

- O*NET electricians' work context: https://www.onetonline.org/link/result/47-2111.00?c=tk
